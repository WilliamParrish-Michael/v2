01 — About

A materials company, focused on consistency and documentation.

Kathors Peptides is a United States–based supplier of research-grade peptides. We concentrate on the parts that matter to a research buyer: consistent material, verified identity, and clear documentation that travels with every lot.

Production is domestic. Each lot is analyzed before release and accompanied by a certificate of analysis, so the material you receive matches what the paperwork says it is. No guesswork, no mystery sourcing.

We describe our products in plain, factual terms — what a compound is, and how it was characterized. We don't make health, performance, or treatment claims, because that isn't what a reference-materials supplier does.

02 — Quality & documentation

Every lot is characterized before it leaves.

  • HPLC

    Purity by chromatography

    Each lot is analyzed by high-performance liquid chromatography to assess purity of the material.

  • MS

    Identity by mass spectrometry

    Molecular weight is confirmed by mass spectrometry so the identity of the peptide is verified, not assumed.

  • COA

    Certificate of analysis

    Documentation is generated per lot and made available to the customer, recording the analytical results for that batch.

  • USA

    Domestic manufacturing

    Material is produced in the United States, keeping the supply chain accountable and traceable.
