Ordering & eligibility

Who we sell to

Kathors supplies verified research organizations only. Accounts are reviewed before any pricing, quoting, or dispatch. We do not sell to individual consumers.

  1. Submit institutional details

    Provide your organization, role, and a work email on an institutional domain.

  2. Verification review

    Our team confirms the account represents a qualified research institution or laboratory.

  3. Purpose attestation

    You confirm materials are for in-vitro research use only — not for human or veterinary administration.

  4. Account activation

    Once approved, pricing and quoting unlock, and orders ship with full lot documentation.
